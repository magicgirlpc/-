# Page topology

1. Dark full-page project background.
2. Rounded white project card with a fixed-position close control.
3. Large 16:9 Mux video preview, autoplaying muted where allowed, with native controls available for full playback and sound.
4. Two-column project information block: large title on the left, description and optional case-study link on the right.
5. Alternating styleframe stack, each image revealed on scroll.
6. Full-viewport dark next-project section with a centered 16:9 thumbnail, small tags, project title, and back-home link.

Desktop uses the reference twelve-column proportions and generous white space. At 900px and below, information becomes a single column, the video becomes shorter, and all imagery spans the card width.
