# Behaviors

- Clicking any spiral or list project opens `/projects/:slug` in the same tab.
- The detail page enters with a soft card rise/fade.
- The hero video autoplays muted and loops. On desktop, the green `play !` tag follows the pointer inside the preview.
- Clicking anywhere in the preview opens a full-viewport player with sound, seeking, fullscreen controls, a close control, and Escape-key dismissal.
- Styleframes reveal with scale and opacity as they enter the viewport. Reduced-motion users see them immediately.
- Close and “back to home” return directly to the originating spiral or list view and skip the entry screen.
- The next-project image navigates to the next project in the project data order.
- Keyboard focus styles remain visible on close, links, video controls, and next-project navigation.
